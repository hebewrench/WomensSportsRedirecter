Chrome browser extension that redirects to display women's sports in search results when gender is not specified.

Browser Extension automatically redirects gender non-specific sports searches to prioritise womens sports and teams. 

Currently works for many European football clubs including those in the WSL, UWCL, Frauen Bundesliga, Liga F, Devision 1 Feminin, Scottish Women's Premier League, and a small selection of other European leagues. Also works for the NWSL, and a large range of Country teams as well (at least the top 118 countries in the FIFA rankings (https://inside.fifa.com/en/fifa-world-ranking/women?) are included currently). 

A few other sports such as rugby, tennis, basketball, cricket, etc also work, i.e. searching "france vs scotland rugby" will redirect to "france vs scotland rugby women". The team names for the top 2 English rugby leagues also work, ie. you can search "Saracens vs bristol bears" and it will redirect to "Saracens vs bristol bears women" for you. Also the teams in the top cricket leagues in England and Australia work too.

There is a suggestion feature for any teams, clubs, sports, or countries that you think should be included, please feel free to use this and any valid suggestions will be inlcuded as soon as possible.
